## Admission process (as published)
1. Counselling and inquiry at the college office or admission desk.
2. Eligibility. +2: SEE or equivalent with required GPA and subject prerequisites. Bachelor's: +2 or equivalent in a relevant stream, per university rules.
3. Application with transcripts, character certificate, recent photographs, copy of citizenship.
4. Selection: merit-based review per board or university rules; some programmes may add an interview or orientation counselling.
5. Scholarships: merit- and need-based. "Up to 50% for Dalit students" after verification. Also aimed at economically weaker, Janajati and other marginalised students.
6. Enrolment: fee formalities and an orientation session to finalise subject choices and class schedules.
